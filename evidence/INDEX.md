## Note on synthetic data

All member names and balances (Alex Morgan, Jordan Lee, Riley Chen, and their dollar amounts)
are synthetic fixtures defined in `demo_app/app.py` — not real people or real financial data.
The redaction checker guards against real secrets/credentials/PII shapes (API keys, auth
headers, cookies, SSNs); it does not flag these fictional business records, which the project's
own ground rules explicitly call for using in place of real customer data.
